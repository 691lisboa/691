FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your pickup location, destination, date and time. The driver replies directly.

Is the booking instant?

A booking request is subject to confirmation and availability. We confirm with you on WhatsApp.

How is the fare calculated?

By the taximeter, under the official taxi tariff in force. The final fare is the amount shown at the end of the trip, except where the law provides otherwise.

Do you travel outside Lisbon?

Yes: Sintra, Fátima, Nazaré, Porto, Évora and other destinations. For longer trips, contact us in advance to confirm availability.

Can I book an airport pickup?

Yes. Tell us your flight and arrival time, your destination and the number of passengers and bags so we can plan the pickup.

Do you speak English?

Yes. You can message us in English on WhatsApp.
