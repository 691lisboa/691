Frequently asked questions

Can we make stops on the way?

You can ask. We arrange the route and stops with you before the trip.

Do you do same-day return trips?

Yes, subject to confirmation. Tell us the destination, departure time and when you want to come back.

How is the fare calculated?

By the taximeter, under the official taxi tariff in force. The final fare is the amount shown at the end of the trip, except where the law provides otherwise.
