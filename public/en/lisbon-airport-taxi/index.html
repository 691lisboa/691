FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take from the airport to central Lisbon?

In normal conditions, about 20 to 30 minutes, depending on traffic and your destination.

What should I send?

Date and time, pickup location or destination, number of passengers and bags and, for arrivals, your flight and expected time.

How is the fare calculated?

By the taximeter, under the official taxi tariff in force. The final fare is the amount shown at the end of the trip, except where the law provides otherwise.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Send us the traveller’s name and contact and the meeting point.
